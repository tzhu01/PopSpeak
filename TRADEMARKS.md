# Trademark and Brand Policy

The OSI-approved MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) grants broad rights to the
source code, including modification, commercial use and redistribution. This
brand policy does not narrow, condition or replace those code rights. It only
addresses the separate question of whether a build, service or commercial
offering may be represented as an official PopSpeak release.

“PopSpeak”, its application icon and other project branding identify the
official project. Forks may accurately state that they are based on PopSpeak,
but should use a distinct product name and icon unless they have written
permission from the brand owner. An official signed PopSpeak binary is a release
identity, not an extra restriction on source-code use. Redistributions must
preserve all notices required by MIT and applicable third-party licenses and
must not misrepresent who built, signed or supports the binary.

This policy does not limit nominative use, commentary, compatibility statements
or other uses permitted by applicable law. It also does not imply endorsement
by the Open Source Initiative.
